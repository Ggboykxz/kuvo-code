# Kuvo Code 0.5.69 — the conversation at rest, and a turn at its end

- **An empty conversation asks the Mac's own question.** A session with nothing in it yet
  was a blank pane; now the Mac's view stands over the transcript while the rows and the
  waiting are both empty (`TranscriptView.swift:69-75`): the two chat bubbles drawn at
  34 pt in the light weight, in the tertiary ink, over "What should we work on in
  `<folder>`?" at `.title3` (15 pt on macOS) in the medium weight, a spacing of 10, lifted
  from the bottom by 120 — the Mac's `plain` form (`TranscriptView.swift:1757-1767`). The
  folder goes by its last path component, however the engine names the path.
- **A turn the engine has finished is not running.** The measure of "the agent is at work"
  ends on the message's own state, as the Mac's does (`Message.isRunning`): a reply the
  engine had stamped finished no longer reads as under way while its session flag still
  says active and the reply carries no parts — the Stop button used to stay over a turn
  that was over. Only a run with no assistant message at all is the engine's own answer
  alone.
- **The live test, meeting a lingering run, asks the engine once more.** If the engine's
  own history says the turn ended, the page reloads and must come back settled; if the
  engine's history still has the message unfinished, that is the engine's lag, and the app
  waits the way the Mac waits.

201 unit tests pass, `tsc --noEmit` is clean, the page builds, and the live page against
the real engine passes in Chromium — the empty conversation's question appears over a
fresh session and steps aside once the words arrive.
