# Kuvo Code 0.5.78 — the appearance's own choices, and two things the app was slow to see

- **The appearance holds the Mac's choices** (`Look.swift:42-63`;
  `AppearanceSettings.swift:9-77`): *Window* — *Glass* ("Frosted glass that lets your
  desktop’s colors through") or *Solid* ("A solid background, with nothing showing
  through"); *Typeface* — System, Rounded, Serif or Monospaced ("Code always stays
  monospaced"); and *Agent’s steps* — *Each Step*, *Summarized* or *Minimal*, each with the
  Mac's own note. The material is the glass the port already drew, or a solid ground; the
  typeface is the messages' (the chrome keeps its own); the steps show as they were
  chosen — a line that opens to each, or only how many there were. Windows has no SF Pro
  Rounded: the closest it has is Candara, whose terminals are round.
- **A session's name shows as soon as the engine has it.** The engine names a session a
  moment after its first words, and the conversation's card said "New Session" until the
  list was read again; the session in view takes its name from the list now.
- **A file picked before the session was in view is not thrown away.** The files in the
  box belong to the session they were picked for, and the draft of another one does not
  take them away.

233 unit tests pass — the appearance's choices, proved without a window — `tsc --noEmit` is
clean, the page builds, and the live page against the real engine passes in Chromium,
twice.
